1. TIEN ICH NAY LAM GI?
----------------------------------------------------------------

Ban co bao gio sua thu mot con so, mot dong chu tren trang web
de xem no trong nhu the nao chua? Kieu doi "5.000" thanh "600.050"
de chup man hinh, hoac de xem bo cuc co vo khong?

Van de: chi can bam F5 la tat ca bay sach.

Tien ich nay ghi nho nhung gi ban sua, roi TU DONG SUA LAI sau
moi lan tai trang. Ban sua mot lan, no giu mai.

QUAN TRONG - doc ky cho nay:

  Nhung thay doi nay CHI HIEN TREN MAY BAN. Khong ai khac nhin
  thay. Khong co gi duoc gui len mang. Trang web that su khong
  he bi thay doi - ban chi dang doi cai hinh anh hien ra tren
  man hinh cua chinh minh.

  Vi vay dung dung no de lam bang chung, de khoe so lieu voi
  sep, hay de chup man hinh gui cho khach hang. Do la lam gia.


----------------------------------------------------------------
2. CAI DAT (chi lam 1 lan)
----------------------------------------------------------------

  1. Mo trinh duyet Chrome.

  2. Go vao thanh dia chi:      chrome://extensions
     Roi bam Enter.

  3. Goc tren ben phai co cong tac "Developer mode" (Che do nha
     phat trien). Bat no len.

  4. Bam nut "Load unpacked" (Tai tien ich da giai nen) vua hien
     ra o goc tren ben trai.

  5. Tim den thu muc du an, chon thu muc ten la:   dist
     Bam "Select Folder".

  6. Xong. Ban se thay bieu tuong tien ich xuat hien tren thanh
     cong cu (goc tren ben phai Chrome).

Neu khong thay bieu tuong: bam vao hinh manh ghep o goc tren ben
phai, tim "DOM Modifier", bam vao hinh dinh ghim de gan no ra
ngoai cho de bam.


----------------------------------------------------------------
3. DUNG LAN DAU - LAM THEO TUNG BUOC
----------------------------------------------------------------

BUOC 1: Mo trang web ban muon sua

  Mo binh thuong nhu moi ngay.

  LUU Y: Neu trang da mo TU TRUOC khi ban cai tien ich, phai bam
  F5 mot lan thi tien ich moi hoat dong tren trang do.


BUOC 2: Bat che do ghi

  - Bam vao bieu tuong tien ich tren thanh cong cu.
  - Bam nut "Bat dau ghi thay doi".
  - Nut se chuyen thanh mau do, co chu "Dang ghi - bam de dung".
  - Bieu tuong tren thanh cong cu hien chu REC mau do.

  Cu de bang thong bao do mo hoac dong deu duoc, khong sao.


BUOC 3: Mo cong cu sua trang

  Bam phim F12.

  Mot bang lon se hien ra (thuong o ben phai hoac ben duoi).
  Do la "DevTools" - cong cu co san cua Chrome, khong phai
  cua tien ich nay.

  Trong bang do, bam vao tab ten "Elements".
  Ben trong la toan bo cau truc cua trang web.


BUOC 4: Sua thu mot thu gi do

  Cach de nhat de tim dung cho:

    - Bam chuot phai vao chu ban muon doi (ngay tren trang web).
    - Chon "Inspect" (Kiem tra).
    - Bang Elements se tu nhay den dung dong chua chu do.

  Roi:

    - Bam DUP chuot vao chu do trong bang Elements.
    - Go chu moi.
    - Bam Enter.

  Chu tren trang doi ngay lap tuc.


BUOC 5: Kiem tra tien ich da ghi duoc chua

  Bam lai vao bieu tuong tien ich.

  Ban se thay mot dong moi trong danh sach, dai khai:

      [text]  span "5.000 NT$"
              5.000 NT$  ->  600.050 NT$
              dang ap dung

  Dong chu "dang ap dung" mau xanh nghia la MOI THU DANG TOT.


BUOC 6: Thu bam F5

  Bam F5 de tai lai trang.

  Doi vai giay. Chu ban sua se tu quay lai.

  Mo lai tien ich, ban se thay dong:

      khoi phuc 1 sau khi tai lai

  Vay la xong. Ban vua sua mot lan va no tu giu lai.


----------------------------------------------------------------
4. HAI CACH LUU - KHAC NHAU CHO NAO?
----------------------------------------------------------------

Day la phan hay nham nhat. Doc ky mot lan la hieu.

  BAN NHAP (tu dong, khong can lam gi)

    - Tu luu, khong can bam nut nao.
    - Song qua: bam F5, trang tu tai lai.
    - CHET khi: ban dong tab, hoac tat Chrome.
    - Dung cho: nghich thu, xem thu, viec lam trong hom nay.

  SNAPSHOT (phai tu bam nut "Luu Snapshot")

    - Phai bam nut moi co.
    - Song qua: dong tab, tat may, khoi dong lai Chrome.
    - Con mai den khi ban tu xoa.
    - Dung cho: nhung thay doi ban muon giu lau dai.

CACH NHO DON GIAN:

    Ban nhap  = tam thoi, mat khi dong Chrome.
    Snapshot  = lau dai, khong mat.

    Muon giu qua ngay mai -> PHAI bam "Luu Snapshot".


CACH LUU SNAPSHOT:

  1. Trong tien ich, bam nut "Luu Snapshot" o duoi cung.
  2. Dat ten de sau nay ban con nho (vi du: "Bao cao thang 3").
  3. Bam "Luu snapshot".

  Tu gio no se tu ap dung moi khi ban vao lai trang do.


----------------------------------------------------------------
5. DOC HIEU MAN HINH TIEN ICH
----------------------------------------------------------------

Tien ich co 3 tab o tren cung:

  Record     - noi ban ghi va xem cac thay doi moi
  Snapshots  - danh sach nhung gi da luu lau dai
  Settings   - cai dat


TRONG TAB "Record", moi dong la mot thay doi. Cuoi moi dong co
mot dong chu nho cho biet no dang the nao:

  "dang ap dung"
      Tot. Thay doi dang co hieu luc tren trang.

  "chua tim thay phan tu"
      Tien ich khong tim lai duoc cho can sua tren trang.
      Thuong vi trang da doi bo cuc, hoac phan do chua hien ra.
      -> Xem muc 7.

  "nhieu phan tu giong nhau"
      Tren trang co nhieu cho giong het nhau, tien ich khong dam
      doan cho nao dung nen khong sua gi ca. Nhu vay an toan hon
      la sua nham cho.

  "ap dung loi"
      Co truc trac. Thu xoa dong do va lam lai.

  "dang tat"
      Ban da bo tich o dau dong. Bo tich lai la chay.


CAC DONG CHU KHAC:

  "Da luu nhap luc 14:32:07 - F5 se tu ap lai"
      Yen tam, moi thu da duoc luu.

  "khoi phuc 3 sau khi tai lai"
      Vua tai lai trang va 3 thay doi da duoc dat lai vao cho.

  "3 chua len trang"
      Co 3 thay doi ghi duoc nhung chua hien tren trang.
      Bam vao tung dong de xem ly do.

  "da loc 47 nhieu tu app"
      Tien ich da bo qua 47 thay doi do CHINH TRANG WEB tu tao ra
      (quang cao tu doi, dong ho chay, so lieu tu cap nhat...).
      Con so nay cang lon cang tot - nghia la no dang loc sach.


CHAM TRON MAU o cuoi moi dong = do chac chan:

  xanh la  - chac chan la ban sua
  vang     - khong chac lam
  do       - nhieu kha nang la trang tu doi, khong phai ban


----------------------------------------------------------------
6. CAC NUT KHAC
----------------------------------------------------------------

  "Chon tat ca" / "Bo chon"
      Bat hoac tat cung luc moi thay doi trong danh sach.

  O tich dau moi dong
      Tat rieng mot thay doi. Tat la no bien khoi trang ngay.
      Tich lai la no quay ve. Dung de so sanh truoc/sau.

  Hinh cai dich (o cuoi moi dong)
      Bam vao se to sang cho do tren trang, de ban biet no o dau.

  Hinh thung rac
      Xoa han dong do.

  "Xoa het"
      Xoa toan bo danh sach dang co. Khong lay lai duoc.

  "Ap dung lai" (thanh duoi cung)
      Bat tien ich thu sua lai ngay. Dung khi trang doi bo cuc
      va thay doi cua ban bi mat.

  Nut mui ten quay lui (thanh duoi cung)
      Tra trang ve nguyen ban ban dau. Khong xoa gi ca -
      bam "Ap dung lai" la moi thu quay tro lai.


----------------------------------------------------------------
7. GAP TRUC TRAC - THU LAN LUOT
----------------------------------------------------------------

TIEN ICH BAO "Khong ket noi duoc voi trang nay"

  Trang da mo tu truoc khi cai tien ich. Bam F5 la xong.

  Neu van khong duoc: tien ich khong chay duoc tren cac trang
  dac biet cua Chrome (dia chi bat dau bang chrome://) va tren
  Chrome Web Store. Do la quy dinh cua Chrome, khong sua duoc.


SUA XONG MA DANH SACH TRONG KHONG

  - Ban da bam "Bat dau ghi thay doi" chua? Kiem tra nut co dang
    mau do khong.
  - Thu sua lai lan nua, cham rai hon.
  - Vao tab Settings, muc "Muc loc khi ghi", doi thanh
    "Tat ca - khong loc". Roi thu lai.


CO TRONG DANH SACH NHUNG TRANG KHONG DOI GI

  Nhin dong chu nho cuoi moi dong (xem muc 5) de biet ly do.

  Neu ghi "chua tim thay phan tu":
    - Doi vai giay, no van dang thu tim.
    - Bam nut "Ap dung lai" o thanh duoi.
    - Cuon den cho co phan tu do roi bam "Ap dung lai".

  Neu cham mau vang hoac do:
    Vao Settings, tat cong tac "Chi ap lai thay doi do ban sua".
    Neu sau do no len -> tien ich da cham nham diem tin cay.


F5 XONG BI MAT

  - Vao Settings, kiem tra "Tu luu & khoi phuc thay doi chua luu"
    phai dang BAT.
  - Neu ban vua dong tab hoac tat Chrome thi ban nhap mat that -
    do la dung thiet ke. Lan sau nho bam "Luu Snapshot".


SUA O VUONG NHAP LIEU (o dien ten, dien so) KHONG AN

  Cai nay hien tai CHUA LAM DUOC. Khong phai ban lam sai.

  Ly do ky thuat: gia tri dang hien trong o nhap lieu khong nam
  trong cau truc trang theo cach ma tien ich co the nhin thay.
  Day la tinh nang chua co, khong phai loi.


TRANG WEB CAP NHAT GIAO DIEN MOI, MOI THU HONG HET

  Binh thuong. Khi chu trang web thay doi cach dung trang, cac
  diem danh dau cu khong con dung nua.

  Cach xu ly: xoa snapshot cu, sua lai tu dau.


----------------------------------------------------------------
8. NHUNG DIEU NEN BIET TRUOC
----------------------------------------------------------------

  - Chi minh ban thay. Khong ai khac thay. Khong gui len mang.

  - Trang web khong he bi sua that. Ban chi dang doi hinh anh
    hien tren man hinh may minh.

  - Dung de lam gia so lieu, gia bang chung, gia anh chup man
    hinh gui nguoi khac. Do la lua doi.

  - Tren nhung trang doi noi dung lien tuc (Facebook, bang so
    lieu tu cap nhat), doi khi thay doi cua ban bi trang ghi de
    trong chop mat roi tien ich sua lai. Nhap nhay mot cai la
    binh thuong.

  - Tien ich cang co it dau hieu de nhan ra cho can sua thi cang
    hay tim nham. Nhung cho co chu rieng, ten rieng thi rat on
    dinh. Nhung con so tran lan giong het nhau thi hay hong.

  - Day la ban dau tien, chua duoc kiem thu ky. Neu gap loi la
    do phan mem chua hoan thien, khong phai do ban dung sai.


----------------------------------------------------------------
9. BANG TRA NHANH
----------------------------------------------------------------

  Muon sua mot chu tren trang
      Bam icon -> Bat dau ghi -> F12 -> tab Elements ->
      bam dup vao chu -> go moi -> Enter

  Muon giu qua ngay mai
      Bam "Luu Snapshot"

  Muon tra trang ve nhu cu
      Bam nut mui ten quay lui o thanh duoi

  Muon bo mot thay doi
      Bo tich o dau dong do (hoac bam thung rac de xoa han)

  Muon tam tat het
      Vao Settings, tat "Guard chong re-render"

  Trang khong doi gi
      Bam "Ap dung lai" o thanh duoi
